A free online study planner for school and everyday life

This public overview introduces a flexible workspace for a student timetable, school tasks, notes and revision tools. Open a quick calculator or another practical tool when the day calls for it, and choose a manageable next step instead of trying to plan every minute.

Plan schoolwork with a weekly student timetable

Make room for a changing week

Keep repeating lessons, one-off tasks, notes, lists and habits in the same browser workspace. The timetable gives you a view of the school week; the task list is for homework, dates and everyday reminders. Update either when plans change. You do not need to fill every space in a calendar to make a plan useful.

Everything Hub is designed for a small next step: write down a task, check today’s lessons, or keep a note where you can find it. Use the free study planner guide for practical ways to organise a week, or visit the student timetable planner guide for supported file-import details.

Use a task list for one-time work

A dated homework task is different from a lesson that repeats every week. Keeping those two kinds of plans in their own views makes it easier to change one without losing the other.

Use free revision tools for active practice

When you are ready to study, choose flashcards, a short quiz or a practice test built from a flashcard set. Books and selected video lessons offer another route into a topic. Try to recall an answer before revealing it, and use explanations to decide what to revisit. Starter materials are introductory rather than complete exam-board coverage, so check your own course specification and teacher guidance.

Everyday tools for school, home and personal plans

A day is more than study. Find a focus timer, calculator, unit converter, bill splitter, QR maker, text tools and other small utilities beside lists, books and saved links. Some tools are designed to process an input in the browser; account progress and external services have their own storage or privacy details. Use the note shown by each feature and avoid entering sensitive information into shared or third-party tools.

See the guide to free everyday online tools for simple one-off jobs and clear limits. The workspace is not a replacement for a full office suite, design service, password manager or school-issued system.
