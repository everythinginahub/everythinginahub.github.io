A study planner, revision toolkit and everyday hub

Everything Hub brings school planning, revision and everyday tools into one online workspace. This page is the public overview; open the interactive workspace whenever you are ready.

Open the Everything Hub workspace.

Keep planning practical

A weekly school timetable, tasks, notes, lists, habits and saved links help put the next thing where you can find it. Use the workspace for a busy school week, personal reminders or a small habit you want to keep visible. Choose one useful action rather than building a schedule that has to be followed perfectly.

Make room for changing plans

Plans change, so you can update a list or move to another tool when the day takes a different shape. Keep school commitments alongside everyday jobs without turning every reminder into a study target. The home screen brings the main tools together and gives each one a clear place.

Revise actively, not just by rereading

For learning, choose flashcards, quizzes and practice tests to retrieve ideas, or explore books and selected video lessons for another explanation. A starter question on the main page offers a short preview. It is only an example: use it as a prompt to practise, then check detailed learning against your teacher’s instructions and course specification.

Try a sample without signing in

The sample question is there to help you see how a short practice session works. There is no score and your answer is not saved. Close it when you have had enough, or return to it later; it is a small introduction rather than a complete lesson or exam course.

Everyday tools belong here too

Study is only one part of a day. Open a timer or calculator, keep a household list, save a useful link, track a habit, or organise reading and notes alongside school tasks. Puzzles and browser games are optional ways to take a break. They sit beside the planning and revision tools, not in place of them.

Search the home screen by the job you have in mind, such as finding a timer, a book or a set of flashcards. Category filters, pins and recent apps can make familiar tools quicker to reach. You can also share a direct link to a particular app and return to the home screen when you want to switch tasks.

Choose your own pace and account options

You can explore the sample before deciding whether to create an account. When you sign in, your lists, habits, notes and timetable can be saved with your account for another visit. Signed-in visits are counted using a browser identifier and recent activity; the landing page explains this before you sign in. No subject selection is required to create an account.

Keep breaks optional

The daily puzzle is untimed and optional, and browser games are simply another part of the hub. There is no need to protect a streak or finish a session before using another tool. Pick one task, try a different feature if it helps, and come back on a schedule that works for you.
