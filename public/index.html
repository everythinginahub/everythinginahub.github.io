A SPACE TO STUDY, PLAN AND DO MORE

A useful hub for school, home and everything between

Everything Hub is an online workspace that puts study planning beside the practical things that fill a day. It brings a weekly school timetable, tasks, notes, lists and habits together with revision activities, reading, games and everyday tools. Start with one small job or browse the app directory when you are ready; you do not have to set up a perfect system before it becomes useful.

Make plans you can actually use

Keep a task list, check the school timetable and jot down a note before an idea disappears. Habit and saved-link tools can keep small routines and useful pages close by. The aim is a clear next step, not a packed schedule: choose what matters today and leave the rest for another time.

Practise, then change approach

Revision includes flashcards, quizzes and practice tests, so you can recall key ideas instead of only rereading them. Browse books and selected video lessons for a different explanation, or use the sample question on this page to see how a short practice session works. Check a resource against your own course or exam specification; the hub is a collection of tools, not a substitute for teacher guidance.

Use the everyday tools too

Planning does not stop at school. Open a timer or calculator, compare grocery unit prices, check a target assessment score, count sentences in a draft, or preview simple Markdown safely. Keep a list, track a habit, save a link, or organise books and notes alongside study tasks. The Life Hacks app gathers practical ideas for study and daily routines; the weekly meal planner gives you a simple place to keep meal ideas you choose. Puzzles and browser games offer an optional change of pace. These areas share one searchable home screen, so you can move between them without treating every part of your day as revision.

Start small and keep control of your pace

Try the sample revision question without an account: your answer is not saved, there is no score, and you can close it whenever you like. When you choose to create an account, your workspace and progress can be saved for later visits. The daily puzzle is optional and untimed; it does not decide whether you can use the other tools.

Use Search apps from Home, type a purpose such as “timer” or “flashcards,” and open the tool that fits. Category filters, pins and recent apps help you return to tools you use often. You can open a shared link to a specific app, then return to the home screen whenever you want to try something else.

Everything Hub is designed for flexible use: school planning on a busy week, a few minutes of revision, a household list, or a quick break. Pick one task, switch tools when your needs change, and come back on your own schedule. There is no streak loss or timed study pressure, and no account is needed to try the sample question.
