Privacy Policy

This page describes what the current Everything Hub website and its connected features do with information. It is a practical description of the implementation, not a promise about the separate services linked or embedded here. Their practices and the site’s features can change.

In brief: you can try the sample question and send website feedback without an account. Feedback messages are stored privately for the site team; no email address is requested. Accounts can save workspace progress to Supabase. A small first-party activity counter runs only for signed-in accounts. The combined AI workspace sends your current question and recent context. AI Studio sends the current prompt and recent context to the selected cloud model (and may fail over to another provider), optionally sends your current question to DuckDuckGo for search, or can send prompts directly to a loopback model server you choose. Avoid entering personal, confidential or sensitive information into feedback or either AI app.

Who provides the site

Everything Hub is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Supabase provides the account, database and server-side function services used by the site. The last-resort cloud fallback uses KindleHub's API at kindlehub-api.arancool3000.workers.dev when `KINDLEHUB_API_KEY` is configured; Google Gemini is tried before KindleHub when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is configured. AI Studio can additionally send cloud prompts to AI Horde at oai.aihorde.net or LLM7 at api.llm7.io; if the site's managed model is configured, it can use KindleHub or Gemini. Optional search queries are sent by the server to DuckDuckGo's HTML search service. A selected local model is contacted directly by your browser at the loopback address you scan. These third parties and local software process data under their own terms, settings and privacy practices. Everything Hub does not control their operation, retention, or availability.

Information used by the site

Accounts and sign-in

Depending on the sign-in method, account information can include a username or email address, authentication credentials, a display name or chat nickname, and selected subjects. Supabase Auth handles passwords and sign-in sessions; passwords are not included in the saved progress record. Username-only accounts use an internal sign-in identifier managed by the account service. Account email addresses are not shown to other people in chat. Username recovery codes are shown when generated; the recovery service stores hashes of those codes rather than the codes themselves.

Saved progress and device storage

When you sign in, the workspace can save lists, habits, tasks, notes, bookmarks, timetable and revision progress, rewards, profile-picture data and other account settings. The main progress record is stored in Supabase, is limited to 100,000 bytes, and is also cached in this browser so it can load and handle interrupted sync. Sync is not end-to-end encrypted; the service operator and service providers may be able to access stored data. Do not save information you would not want the service operator to see.

Some features also keep device-only data such as meal ideas, game scores, app pins and recent items, reading position and settings, and unsynced changes. The weekly Meal Planner stores the ideas you enter in this browser under a key for the signed-in account; they are not synced to Supabase. These local features may be associated with the signed-in account on that device. Signing out does not necessarily erase them. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes local data and identifiers, but does not delete the account or cloud data; unsynced changes may be lost.

Website feedback

If you choose to send feedback, you can do so without signing in. The site stores the category you select, your message and the app section you were viewing, along with a creation time. It does not attach your signed-in account credentials, ask for or store your email address, and feedback is private to the site team. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal details. To limit spam, the server processes the request’s source IP address and uses a keyed, one-way hash for a limit of five submissions per hour; the hash is not an account identifier, and inactive rate-limit rows are cleaned up during later successful submissions after their window is more than 24 hours old. Supabase may also process ordinary request metadata while handling the request. Feedback messages are kept in the project until the site operator deletes them. The form does not promise a personal response.

Community and shared content

If you choose to publish a flashcard set, it is visible to other signed-in users. Community chat messages and the nickname used with them are visible to signed-in users. The current chat code removes messages older than 24 hours during later message activity and caps the stored message list at 1,000; because cleanup runs when messages are sent, a message may remain longer if no later message triggers cleanup. You can delete your own community message in the app. Reports and moderation-related records may also be processed.

Live-game rooms contain the host’s questions, players’ nicknames and answers, and any room-chat messages. Rooms expire after about two hours and are removed during later room creation; related room data is deleted with the room. Expired data may remain until that cleanup runs. Use a nickname that does not identify you, and do not post contact details or other private information.

Signed-in activity counts

The site’s first-party status counter records activity only while an account is signed in. It uses a random browser identifier saved in local storage, a random per-tab session identifier, the signed-in account identifier and timestamps to count page views and recent presence. The private status page is excluded from page-view counts. Its counting function does not store page paths, page text, questions, IP addresses or email-address fields. Supabase may still process ordinary request metadata, such as a source IP address, while serving requests under its own service practices. The browser identifier helps estimate distinct browsers; it is not a reliable count of people and changes if browser storage is cleared.

Session rows older than one day are removed when a later activity ping performs cleanup. Unique-browser identifiers and aggregate totals have no automatic expiry in the current database code. Historical anonymous totals from before anonymous tracking was disabled remain part of the displayed historical counts; new anonymous page views are not collected.

One combined AI workspace

AI chat and AI Studio are now a single workspace at #ai. Existing #kindle-ai, #ai-studio and #abliterated-ai links open it too. All include recent conversation context, where space permits. Automatic routing stays selectable even when model discovery is unavailable; that is not a guarantee that a provider can answer.

AI Studio

AI Studio is a native Everything Hub workspace, independently implemented and inspired by the Abliterated AI Runner; no upstream source code was copied. All AI routes open the combined workspace. Its authenticated Supabase function returns live model catalog entries from AI Horde and LLM7, plus Automatic routing. AI Horde models appear only while the provider reports active workers, a queue below the configured threshold and a recognizable model size of at least 7B. LLM7 requires a server-configured API token and is limited to GPT-OSS 20B/120B and Gemma 4 31B IDs, and only appears when its live model list includes the ID. Smaller or unclassified models are hidden. A catalog listing is not a live generation test; model access, response compatibility, capacity and provider availability can change. Automatic routing tries up to three live cloud models first, then Google Gemini if configured, and KindleHub only as a last resort. A manually selected cloud model is tried first. Alternatives may be from another category or provider. Provider keys remain server-side. Cloud requests can fail over to up to two other live models and then the configured fallback providers; the same prompt may then be sent to a different provider. The model selection, response style and up to eight previous messages (where space permits) are combined with the current message and sent as one prompt. The composer allows 3,500 characters for the current message and the complete prompt is limited to 4,000 characters. Context may be truncated. Remote requests reserve up to 12 attempts per account in a fixed 60-second window; there is no per-account daily site cap, and failed or cancelled calls may count. Supabase stores the account identifier and latest short-term request window start/count, not the prompt or answer. Provider terms, quotas, costs, retention and availability are controlled by those providers. Do not send personal or sensitive information; AI answers can be inaccurate and should be checked.

KindleHub says API calls share the key owner's daily AI allowance with their KindleHub app; its tiers have different daily message, per-key rate and output limits. This site's users share the configured key's allowance. KindleHub prohibits sharing keys. Its retention and availability are outside our control; paid plans may incur charges. Google Gemini has its own terms, model quotas and project billing settings. Studio sessions, drafts, replies and prototype code stay in page memory, are not saved to account progress or browser storage, and are cleared when you leave Studio. The limiter keeps one row per account, deleted when the Auth account is deleted.

Web search is opt-in and off by default. If you enable it, only the current question (up to 240 characters) is sent from the server to DuckDuckGo's HTML search service. Up to five short snippets and HTTPS source links are then sent to the selected cloud model and shown with its answer. Search can fail or return inaccurate or malicious content; treat snippets as untrusted. The search provider may process the query under its own practices.

Local model scanning happens only after you request it. The browser sends a model-list request to the loopback `/v1/models` address you enter; no prompt is included in that scan. If you select a qualifying local model, the prompt goes directly from your browser to that local server, not through Supabase or a cloud model provider. Local requests do not use the site's rate limiter or web search. The server must be running and allow this site's CORS/private-network access. Local software, its configuration and any onward network traffic are outside Everything Hub's control. No small in-browser offline models are bundled in AI Studio.

Web Studio can put HTML that you provide or receive in an inline prototype into an iframe sandbox with an opaque origin, so it cannot read Everything Hub data. Its content policy blocks programmatic network connections and external subresources; browser support for blocking navigations can vary, so avoid following links from a preview. Only run code you have reviewed. Inline scripts can execute within the preview sandbox, and content can display or process information pasted directly into it. The prototype is not published or transmitted by previewing it. Do not paste secrets or personal information into a prototype, and close AI Studio to clear its in-memory code.

Browser storage and third-party services

The site uses browser local storage and session storage for sign-in state, saved or cached progress, the activity identifier, settings and device-only feature data. The core app does not load a third-party analytics SDK. The signed-in counter described above is first-party and uses a local-storage identifier rather than an advertising cookie.

Some features contact external services when you view or use them. The landing-page Launch Orbit, Better Launch and CodeHype badges load images from those providers. Video thumbnails and embedded lessons use YouTube services, including YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player domain. Optional book scans use Internet Archive. Games may load from Geometry Dash, Two Player Games, Classroom Resources, jsDelivr or the independent game collection. Links and saved bookmarks can open other websites. These services receive their own requests and may receive information such as your IP address, browser/device information and a referrer, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Their availability, content and data practices are controlled by those providers, not Everything Hub. Review the relevant provider’s privacy information before using an embed or external link.

Retention and account requests

Cloud progress and account information remain in the connected service until changed or removed by the service operator. The site does not currently provide a self-service account deletion control. AI rate-limit data keeps one latest-window row per account rather than a daily history; it is removed when the associated Auth account is deleted. Provider logs, backups and operational records may have separate retention rules set by the providers. Local browser data remains until it is overwritten or cleared in your browser.

Questions or deletion requests: contact the maintainer through the project’s GitHub page. GitHub issues are public, so do not post your username, email address, recovery code, password, AI prompt or other private account information there. No private account-support or self-service deletion flow is currently provided on this site.
