A practical planner, not another complicated system

Some weeks are predictable; others change around lessons, family plans and deadlines. Everything Hub keeps a task list beside a repeating weekly timetable, so you can see what is coming up without having to turn every hour into a target. Add a date when it helps, or leave a task undated and choose it when you are ready.

Keep the next task visible

Write down a clear next action, add an optional due date, and filter the list when you want to focus on what is starred, unfinished or done.

See your regular week

Record lesson days and start times in a weekly timetable. The home screen can highlight today’s schedule alongside tasks and habits.

Keep notes and checklists nearby

Use short notes for details you want to find again, or make separate lists for kit, shopping and repeated routines.

How to start planning a school week

  1. Choose one place to begin. Add a task you need to remember, or enter the lessons that repeat each week.
  2. Make the plan easy to change. Add dates only where they help. Move or edit a task when plans shift rather than trying to keep a perfect schedule.
  3. Use revision tools when you need them. Open flashcards or a short quiz for a focused practice session; the planner and the study activities sit in the same workspace.
Keep your own course in view. The starter material is introductory and is not complete exam-board coverage. Check detailed revision against your teacher’s guidance and the specification you study.

Study planner questions

Is the Everything Hub study planner free?

The current workspace is free to use and runs in a web browser. You need to sign in to save account progress; no download is required.

Does the planner make a timetable for me?

You add the lessons and tasks that fit your week. It is a flexible organiser, not a school-issued timetable or a replacement for teacher instructions.

Can I use it for revision too?

Yes. The workspace includes flashcards, quizzes, practice tests, books and selected video lessons. See the revision tools guide for how to get started.
